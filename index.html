How to Make More Profit

1. Buy BetterCompare supplier rates, quality and delivery before procurement.
2. Reduce WastageControl cutting, handling, storage, excess issue and damage.
3. Raise ProductivityMeasure daily output per crew and remove idle time.
4. Protect MeasurementCapture approved and executed quantities with records.
5. Reduce ReworkInspect before covering or advancing to the next stage.
6. Control EquipmentTrack utilization, idle time, fuel and productive output.
7. Forecast EarlyUse cost-to-complete to discover leakage before final account.
8. Manage VariationsDocument approved changes and cost/time consequences.
Use verified accounts/ledger/bills for actual profit claims; the calculator is intentionally editable.
